# Brand & Frontend Architecture: Quiet Heritage Golf OS (India)

NEVER use default AI/SaaS styling. Before writing ANY UI, HTML, CSS, React, or slide deck code, you MUST invoke and obey `~/.claude/skills/design-taste-frontend/SKILL.md` and `~/.claude/skills/impeccable/SKILL.md`.

## Mandatory Visual System ("Aman Meets Club Scorecard")
- **Target Audience:** Affluent Indian golfers, HNI members, and prestigious Indian golf club committees.
- **Backgrounds:** Warm Unbleached Limestone (`#F5F1E8`) primary canvas; Travertine Stone (`#EBE5D8`) elevated surfaces; Crisp Paper (`#FAF8F3`) active slot cards. NEVER use pure `#FFFFFF` backgrounds or dark neon themes.
- **Ink & Borders:** Espresso Charcoal (`#181614`) primary text; Warm Graphite (`#5C574F`) secondary text; `1px solid #D6CEBF` architectural hairlines (prefer hairline rules and crisp borders over heavy drop shadows).
- **Accents:** Burnished Terracotta/Brass (`#B05E36`) for primary CTAs and selected tee slots.
- **STRICT BAN ON GOLF CLICHÉS:** Zero turf-green buttons, zero green gradients, zero cartoon golf ball/flag icons, zero centered 3-column Lucide icon grids, zero Inter/Roboto default font stacks. Let authentic course photography provide the only green on the page.
- **Typography:**
  1. Display / Headings: `Instrument Serif` or `Newsreader` (tight tracking `-0.02em`, editorial weight).
  2. UI / Body: `Plus Jakarta Sans` or `Geist`.
  3. Tee Times, ₹ Pricing, Handicaps & 4-Ball Counters: `JetBrains Mono` with `font-variant-numeric: tabular-nums`.
