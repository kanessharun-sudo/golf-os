    Before your first booking

    Do members pay a green fee?

    No. If a player is on the ASC Centre member roll, their green fee is ₹0. Membership is ₹700 a month.

    Can I book for three instead of four?

    Yes. Choose a 3-ball or a 4-ball when you pick your time. The slot is held for that group size.

    Someone else booked me. Will I see the round?

    Yes. Sign in with the name and mobile number used in the booking, and the upcoming round appears with your scorecard link.

    How are caddies assigned?

    The club matches caddies to groups from the day's tee sheet. Your caddie's name appears on the booking once assigned, and both of you check in at the first tee.
