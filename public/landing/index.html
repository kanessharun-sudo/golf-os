    Scoring

    One link per player. No pencil, no arguments.

    When a booking is made, everyone in it gets their own scorecard link. They sign in with the name and mobile number used at booking, and the round is waiting for them.

    1. Booked. The slot appears for every player, whoever made the booking.
    2. Playing. Each player enters their own strokes, hole by hole.
    3. Finished. Net scores and handicaps update when the card is signed.

    Questions

    Before your first booking.

    Do members pay a green fee?

    No. If a player is on the ASC Centre member roll, their green fee is ₹0. Membership is ₹700 a month.

    Can I book for three instead of four?

    Yes. Choose a 3-ball or a 4-ball when you pick your time. The slot is held for that group size.

    Someone else booked me. Will I see the round?

    Yes. Sign in with the name and mobile number used in the booking, and the upcoming round appears with your scorecard link.

    How are caddies assigned?

    Caddies see the day's tee sheet and are matched to groups by the club. Your caddie's name appears on the booking once assigned.
